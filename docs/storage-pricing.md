# Storage pricing

Charges apply for each full 24 hours a package stays in a locker. **Anything under 24 hours is free.**

| Storage period | Daily rate |
| -------------- | ---------: |
| First 5 days   |  100 cents |
| Next 5 days    |  200 cents |
| Day 11 onward  |  300 cents |

For example,

6 days and 12 hours costs **700 cents**:

500 for the first five days and 200 for the sixth.

The extra 12 hours are not charged.
